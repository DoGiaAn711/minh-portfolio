002 · About

I film sport like cinema, not coverage.

I'm AnDo — a sport videographer based in Hà Nội. I started with a passion for sport and a camera, and spent three years embedded with Hanoi Buffaloes — shooting everything from game-day hype reels to player documentaries.

My work lives in the small things — the squeak of sneakers before tip-off, tape on a boxer's knuckles, the backhand dink at match point. I grade in DaVinci and believe sound is half the film.
